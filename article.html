The Featurization Service in Content Analytics

Featurization is how Content Analytics turns your images and assets into structured, measurable attributes. This lesson explains what it does and why it matters.

What featurization does

When a page loads, Content Analytics collects the URLs of the images on it. Adobe's cloud then fetches each asset and runs it through the featurization service, which uses AI to detect visual attributes such as objects, colors, and style of photography. (Assets are images; the text on a page is analyzed separately as experience attributes.)

Why it matters

These detected features become dimensions you can report on. Instead of only knowing which page performed well, you learn what kind of content drives engagement — for example, that images with people outperform product-only shots.

Tracking clickthroughs

Featurization tells you what an asset contains; clickthroughs tell you whether people act on it. The image above is wrapped in a link marked with a data-asset-click attribute. When you click it, our data-layer script records an Asset Click event and then sends you on to Lesson 2.

In Content Analytics you can pair that click metric with the features detected on the asset — so you can answer questions like "do images with people earn more clickthroughs?" Click the image, or this link, to continue →

The one requirement

Featurization runs on Adobe's servers, so every asset URL must be publicly reachable over HTTPS. Local addresses like localhost or 127.0.0.1 cannot be fetched, so asset-level data will be missing until the page is served from a public URL. Note too that featurization currently supports English only.
