Walkthrough · Lesson 2

From Asset to Conversion

In Lesson 1 the featurization service told us what an asset contains and a click told us people were interested. This lesson connects that asset to a conversion — so you can prove content drives outcomes, not just engagement.

The two conversion actions

Above are two calls to action. Join the Club is a soft conversion (a lead); Book Your Stay is a hard conversion (an order). Each button fires a data-layer event — cta.register or cta.buy — and the booking carries an order with a unique ID, price, and currency.

How the conversion links to the asset

The conversion doesn't stand alone. When this page loaded, Content Analytics recorded an impression of the asset (the image) and its experience (the page), each with a stable identity. The click and the conversion event are collected in that same page context, so Content Analytics can tie them all together:

Asset / experience impression → asset click → conversion (register / order)

Because every step shares that identity, the order is attributed to the exact content that was seen and clicked — not just counted in isolation. That link is what lets you say a specific asset or experience drove the conversion.

Does this content lead to conversions — not just engagement?

With that link in place, engagement (impressions, clicks) and outcome (registrations, orders) sit on the same row in your reports — so you can move past "which asset got attention" to "which asset actually moved people to act."

What is the ROI on this content?

Every cta.buy sends an order value into the dataset. Divide that revenue by the cost of producing and running the asset and you have content-level ROI: this image earned N orders worth $X. That is the full journey — from a featurized asset, to a clickthrough, to a measurable conversion and its return.
